## Intro/overview

Overview of our Brainstorming, and Mock-ups of our automatic pet feeder. Each concept is based on user needs we gathered from product reviews and the Mock-ups below can show different ways our design could make feeding easier, more reliable, and more convenient for pet owners. 

## Generating Ideas

[Generating Ideas](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JsgU43wR2RqLS_dOugLTlFD9ctC5pSoCEJuoboHtt4s/edit?usp=sharing)

Above is a link to our Generated Ideas list where we took the most important and common user needs and we compiled a list of 100 features that could potentially be used to satisfy the needs with a detail description on what it is or what the function of the feature is.


## Step Three

[Ranking Features](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14rYSPKzU6le_88QI3B5IAq3_B2Lat62RiwGScZTgRo8/edit?usp=sharing)

Above is a ink to our ranking of the features we organized them into categories ranking them on a scale of P10-P1 (P10 being the most important to our team and P1 being the least) with this we then went to rank each feature in their categories with a sub ranking lower numbers meaning they are features we are interested in or think that we would be able to add to our design, higher meaning were not to sure about the incorporation of the feature.
