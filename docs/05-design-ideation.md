## Intro/overview

Overview of our Brainstorming, and Mock-ups of our automatic pet feeder. Each concept is based on user needs we gathered from product reviews and the Mock-ups below can show different ways our design could make feeding easier, more reliable, and more convenient for pet owners.

## Step Five

  For this part of the project we had to brainstorm, design and model the device we are going to build. This process happened after the research was done so we have a good idea what we are aiming for. To start things off, each member came up with about thirty four possible different features. These features were based on the need and requirements we had researched in the early stages of the project. In total there were 100 features.

  After identifying the features we ranked in their separate categories. The categories are based on safety, customization, ease of use, accuracy and notifications. Each category is ranked with P1 being the best through P10 being the worst. For each feature under a category is ranked from best to worst feature in said category. These rankings are based on what we wanted in our own pet feeder. With this ranking we can pick which features to use and not to use. Do note lower ranking can still be used in the device design.

  Once the rankings were finalized, the team reviewed the top features from each category together to make sure our priorities lined up before moving into the design phase. This discussion helped clarify which features were considered essential versus which ones were simply nice to have, and it gave each team member a clearer target to design around. Comparing notes at this stage also surfaced a few disagreements about priorities, which we resolved by revisiting our original research and requirements to confirm which features actually addressed the core problems we were trying to solve. This step ensured that even though each member would go on to create their own independent design, all three designs would still be grounded in the same shared set of priorities.

  With the feature ranked now we can now start designing the product. This is not going to be the final version of the product, more of a rough draft to show what it would look like. Each team member is responsible for making a 3D design of their own pet feeder with the goal to have 3 different designs to choose from. They are also responsible for explaining how their model works in detail.
