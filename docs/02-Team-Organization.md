## Team Charter

Team 206 aims to design and manufacture an Automatic Pet Feeder that delivers precise measured meals on a set schedule. Our mission is to serve pet owners with busy work days, long commutes, or travel plans, giving their pets steady, healthy feeding routines even when no one is home. Because owners trust the feeder with their pet’s well-being, it has to work every time, with no hidden gaps. We hold ourselves to that standard through consistent effort and accountability. We value transparency and raise problems early instead of letting them go unspoken. We treat each other with respect and share a commitment to durability, so that what we build holds up under real daily use and under scrutiny. When challenges come up, we work through them openly and together, driven by a shared standard of excellence and pride in the product we deliver.

## Product Mission Statement

Our mission on team 206 is to design and manufacture a compact and reliable device that can sense real world input, be able to process it, and respond with a purposeful action to solve meaningful everyday problems.
