## Meeting Coordination

1. The team will set reminders for upcoming meetings, and the Meeting Leader will send a reminder message a day prior and the day of the meeting. 
1. Meeting Leader will reach out to the team when organizing meetings to confirm that members can attend and will come up with an alternate time if there is a conflict with the usual meeting times.
1. The team will aim to have face-to-face meetings, but if the teams schedule will not allow it then there will be a zoom meeting held in place of an in-person meeting.
1. Notices for not being able to attend an in-person meeting should be given five hours prior to the meeting to give the other members time to decide whether to move meeting time, have a hybrid meeting where the member can attend via zoom, or procced without the member (Possible Exceptions: Family or Medical Emergency).

1. Roles will be changed every two weeks on Wednesday.
2. The way roles will be changed will look like this, the Meeting Leader becomes Meeting Recorder and Assignment Leader, and then the person who was those two roles is now the Project Monitor then back to Meeting Leader to ensure each member will have multiple opportunities with each role.
3. Members can inform each other either through messages or during our meetings to make sure they understand their tasks and responsibilities and assist them if they seem they need help understanding what to do.
4. Outside of our scheduled two-week shift of team roles and someone communicates with the team they would want to change roles for the betterment of the team then that is a desicion the team can make together. If the group needs to switch roles twice due to lack of a member participating in their assigned role, then the team will have to have a discussion with the instructor.
5. Milestones and Activities should be tracked by the Project Monitor and their main source of checking milestones will be due dates for assignments or following the teams Gantt chart to ensure they are on track with their goals.
6. Technical Responsibilities will be decided based on members individuals strengths. If they feel confident in a task, then that member will be assigned to that certain tasks if all members are unsure then that will be assigned as a group task that all or some members can do together.

## Team Coordination & Accountablity

1. The team will aim to have team assignments done by 6:00 pm on the day that an assignment is due to give all members enough time to look over the assignment and make any last-minute changes prior to the due date
2. When assignment tasks are divided this is the opportunity for the team member to voice if they need assistance or feel they are not suitable for the task if this is the case then tasks can be shifted or they can get guided assistance so that each team member will contribute equally.
3. Feedback from the design review will be acted on by the members whose section was given feedback and will update their part with the recommended revisions. And any group feedback will be split evenly among the group to update the team's design.
4. Missed tasks from a group member will be marked by the other members of the team and if there was plenty of time and no emergency the member will get a verbal warning. If it occurs a second time, then the group will bring this to the instructor's attention to disccus how to procced.
5. Team members will check in prior to the assignment due date to make sure members are on track to complete their work and make sure they have a plan for completion and have a plan to get their action item done.
6. If the team realizes a member is underperforming or givng under quality work there will need to be a disscussion with the team to brainstorm how the underperforming member can improve their work and maybe give advice or sceduling a meeting with the grader or instructor to see how they can better their work.
7. If there are no signs of any improvement with an underperforming team member after there's been a discussion on the matter and how they can be helped and they aren't constantly making an effort to meet with the team, instructors, or graders to improve then the other team members may bring up the possibilities of a pink slip if the quality of the project would improve with the removal of said member.


## Conflict Recognition & Resolution

1. In the event of conflict the members should attempt to resolve it with each other by reaching out to the person they are having the problem with and discuss their issues and try to work on resolving the issue, if either this doesn't work or the member having issues is nervous to bring it to the attention of the other party then the third member can act as a mediator for the two and attempt to resolve the issue. If the conflict to still is ongoing between the two members or the conflict is between all three members and they simply can't resolve it then an instructor will be asked to be involved to see how the team can procced.
